    Invoice layouts it reads

    Five vendors, five genuinely different documents. If every sample used one template the extraction step would be filling in a form, not reading an invoice - so each one hides the same facts somewhere else.

    What every invoice is checked against

    An invoice on its own proves nothing. Each of the nine checks compares it against a specific piece of reference data, and all of it is here: who we are allowed to pay, what was ordered, what has already been billed, and the thresholds that decide how much variance is acceptable.

    Approved vendors

    The list of suppliers we are allowed to pay at all. An invoice from anyone else, or from a blocked vendor, cannot be paid.
